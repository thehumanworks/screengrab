screengrab capture for vision-capable LLMs

Set up your capture

screengrab records the screen at a fixed FPS and writes individual PNGs or a spritesheet. The defaults are tuned for handing the result to a vision-capable LLM, so the frame rate is intentionally low.

Pick a physical display, or a windowed application (including a maximized app on its own Space — those normally need a swipe to reach).

Microphone and transcription are off by default. Audio stays on this Mac; transcription never falls back to a cloud service.
